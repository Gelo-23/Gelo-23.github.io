Our First Date

Our first met, I picked her up at Caltex Fortune to take her to the LRT Antipolo station. After I got home and was getting ready for school, she messaged me saying she had gotten off at LRT Katipunan because she almost fainted, so I quickly got ready to go to her. When I arrived, I accompanied her to her university. After dropping her off and finishing our class, we went to a restaurant in Mendiola to eat and have coffee while kwentuhan. Afterward, we went to my house so I could get my motorcycle to take her home; we were just joking around and having fun during the ride. We had planned to stop by a photobooth she knew, but it turned out it wasn't there anymore, so I just dropped her off at the Caltex Fortune near her place.
